01 — Our Story

A Legacy of Music & Discipline

Our brass band was founded by Bishop Lucky Molotsane, inspired by his father Bishop Christopher Molotsane during the 1980s, where he participated in his father's brass band and gained vast experience till today.

Our brass band comprises of boys and girls between the ages of 10 and 21 as well as adults from 21 to 35, enlisted from schools, churches and families in and around our local communities in Pretoria as well as surrounding provinces.

03 — Our Journey

Our History

1980s

The Beginning

Bishop Christopher Molotsane starts a brass band, inspiring his son Lucky to carry the torch of music and discipline.

2000s

Molo Brass Band Founded

Bishop Lucky Molotsane officially establishes Molo Brass Band, growing from a small community group into a recognized performing ensemble.

2022

Ford Motor Company Partnership

Ford sponsors instruments and uniforms, marking a major milestone in the band's growth and professional recognition.

Today

Growing Stronger

100+ members, 500+ performances, and a mission to impart discipline and responsibility to youth through music and dance.
